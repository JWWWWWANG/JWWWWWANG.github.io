Epochs

[011]2024AIGC

Epochs traces the grand narrative of existence through a visual journey that connects cosmic, geological, and biological evolution. The film navigates through pivotal moments of transformation—from the universe's explosive birth to humanity's first discoveries—exploring how each epoch laid the foundation for the next.

Utilizing advance AI software like Midjourney, Runway, Pika, Elevnlabs, and ChatGPT, this 1 minute short film invites viewers to consider their place within this continuing story of transformation, presenting evolution not as a series of isolated events, but as a single, flowing narrative of constant becoming.

Collaborated with Anna Chen, Alen Zhang, and Lydia Peng.

Credit: Chatgpt, Midjourney, Runway, Pika, Elevenlabs.
