Jiawen Wang

Reborn

[008]2024Audio-Visual / Video Art

Reborn is a real-time audiovisual performance that chronicles a personal journey of artistic transformation through the symbolic language of Tarot cards. Structured as a three-act narrative, the piece uses The Death, The Wheel of Fortune, and The Magician cards to frame pivotal moments of creative evolution and internal conflict.

Throughout the performance, abstract storytelling techniques merge with real-time manipulation to create a visceral experience of artistic metamorphosis. The work's live nature emphasizes the immediacy of creative transformation, while its use of Tarot symbolism connects personal experience to universal themes of change, resistance, and renewal.
